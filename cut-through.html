The Map

The frequency bands — what each one controls

Every instrument has a home in specific bands, and every band has a job. Knowing the map is the difference between guessing and deliberately shaping a source.

Golden rule: cut before you boost. Cutting overlapping frequencies is almost always cleaner than boosting the other signal.

The Problem

Frequency masking — why instruments get lost

When two sounds occupy the same frequency range at once, the louder one makes the quieter one hard — or impossible — to hear. A muddy mix and a buried vocal are almost always masking.
