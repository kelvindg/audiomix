Phase 01 · The Rig

What you need, and how to wire it

Open Sound Meter (OSM) compares two sounds: the pink noise before it hits the speaker, and the same noise after it comes out — picked up by a mic. That comparison is the measurement. Gear is simple: any audio interface, a measurement mic, and your laptop.

Audio interface — anything works; the video uses a Focusrite Scarlett Measurement microphone — a calibration mic (e.g. Rational Acoustics RTA-420) Pink noise — OSM’s built-in generator

The signal chain

Generator → speaker → mic → interface, with the reference looped back inside OSM before it ever reaches the speaker.

Phase 02 · The Signal

Route OSM so it compares the right things

OSM’s trick: click any module — graph, measurement, or generator — and its settings appear at the bottom of the screen. Nothing is hidden in menus. Get the measurement channel on the mic, the reference on the loop, levels matched, and the delay compensated.

Save, label, and keep every measurement
